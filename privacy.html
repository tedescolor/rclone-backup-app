Privacy policy

This app is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • No data is collected, stored, shared or sold by the app or its author.
  • Files are copied only between the owner's computer and the owner's own Google Drive. No data is sent to any other server.
  • The access token stays on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
